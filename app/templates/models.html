Metrics

How to read these numbers
  • R² – the share of price differences the model explains. 1.0 is perfect, 0 means no better than always guessing the average price.
  • MAE – the average size of the error, in price units. Lower is better.
  • RMSE – like MAE, but big mistakes count more.
  • MAPE – the average error as a percentage of the real price.
  • Test split – one 80/20 split, evaluated on only a small group of houses, so the numbers are a bit noisy.
  • Cross-validation (CV) – the data is split 5 ways and repeated 3 times, and the scores are averaged. It is more stable, so use it to compare models. The ± value shows how much the score changes between splits.
  • Green marks the better model in each row.
